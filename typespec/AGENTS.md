ownership, compatibility, normalization, validation, and tooling; plus
[`../agent-guides/go.md`](../agent-guides/go.md) or

companion file changes.

subscription, or payment contracts, also read
[`../agent-guides/hub-subscriptions.md`](../agent-guides/hub-subscriptions.md).
